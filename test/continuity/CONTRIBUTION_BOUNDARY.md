# Contribution boundary

GovKM is contributing only the synthetic scenario encoded in `operative-state.test.ts` and the explanatory text in this directory.

Included:

- synthetic identifiers and facts;
- one operative-state invariant;
- expected current and historical outcomes.

Not included:

- GovKM continuity ontology;
- continuity scoring/ranking methodology;
- proprietary test-generation logic;
- private research fixtures;
- organizational continuity architecture;
- unpublished evaluation data.

This boundary is informational and does not modify the Nocetta CLA.
